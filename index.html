Kun Yang

Machine Learning Engineer at Google · Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Google in Sunnyvale, CA, focusing on LLM inference efficiency, self-improving multi-agent code optimization, and reinforcement learning environments for post-training and evaluation.

Previously, I served as a Senior Data Science Engineer at Juniper Networks and earned my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Virginia (UVA) advised by Prof. Cong Shen. Prior to UVA, I received my M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Texas A&M University (advised by Prof. Tie Liu) and my B.E. in Electrical and Information Science from Tsinghua University. My research spans multi-agent systems, bandit & prompt optimization, offline reinforcement learning, and federated learning.
